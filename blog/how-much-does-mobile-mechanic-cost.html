Is a Mobile Mechanic More Expensive?

One of the first questions people ask: "Is it more expensive since they come to you?" The short answer: no. In most cases, mobile mechanics cost the same as or less than traditional shops — and you skip the tow truck, waiting room, and wasted day.

Why Mobile Service Often Costs Less Overall

Lower overhead: No building lease, utility bills, or waiting room.

No tow fees: We come to where your car is — even if it won't start.

Your time back: Work, relax, or watch us work instead of sitting at a shop all day.

Efficiency: We book back-to-back with no idle bay time, which keeps quotes competitive.

What Affects Your Quote

  • Year, make, and model of your vehicle
  • Parts needed (OEM vs quality aftermarket options)
  • Access difficulty and labor time
  • Whether diagnosis finds related issues worth fixing now
